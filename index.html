The problem

You can't hunt a season you missed.

A hunt takes months of getting ready — scouting, permits, points, gear, time off. But every one of those months sits on top of a handful of dates: the application window, the draw, the opener. Miss one, and the season is over before it starts. Open Season keeps track of the dates that matter, so the preparation counts.
